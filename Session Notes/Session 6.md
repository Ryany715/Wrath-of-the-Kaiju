The players continued their descent into the earth towards the aquifer that lies beneath Kihoso. They first came across a room of strange rock creatures who seemed to all be huddled around working on something. They continued past these creatures and found a shallow flowing river the leading deeper into the earth. They found 2 kappa wading through the river and decided to barter a bit with them, Kraige having a kitsune mask of particular interest. Continuing on they encountered some Kabuto, who they quickly dispatched, and found themselves along the shoreline of the massive aquifer. The party found Yoshito with his large Kabuto, "The Decapitator" along the shores of the aquifer pointed at attention towards a massive eggs that seems to have hatched. Dr. Dolots explained that she believes the device will work in these waters but it will take about 12 hours for it to kick into full effect. The party beds down for the night, deep below the surface of the earth.

# Preamble(Wrath of the Kaiju Homebrew)

[[Shen Ji]] is a mysterious realm surrounded by a strange swirling mist that seems to blur the veil between the spirit realm and prime material plane. This realm is filled with yokai, beings of spiritual energy that take enumerable forms, with enumerable goals. The fiercest and most godlike of these yokai are the kaiju, embodiments of nature and magic, who shape the very earth they walk upon. The kaiju of Shen Ji define the epochs of history, periods of peace and tranquility known to following death of a kaiju. It is here that our story starts, at the seeming end of these periods of peace and prosperity. After the[[Lei Shen#The Hunt of Thunder| Hunt of Thunder]] some 1000 years ago, the last great emperor of Shen Ji ushered in a millennium of prosperity and growth. In the past year, Shen Ji has begun to shake under the mighty weight of kaiju once again. It has been 1000 years since the last kaiju was taken care of, some are not even sure they can be killed.

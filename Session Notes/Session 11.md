The swords of Kihoso arrived in the ringlanders town in the Tetsumine Basin. Exploring the town they found a Captain Totsuka who implored the party for aid in investigating the missing monks who had sailed for the Ironspire, and warned the party of the incoming Rising Blossoms and the SIlk purse. They sought the patronage of Kentucky Greg who haggled with them over the price of a treasure map of copper spire number 13. The swords boarded Bo's Big Boats and set out towards the copper spire. 

Bo took the party to the copper spire, and assured them he would return when they phoned via the sending stone they left with him. The party continued into the spire, discovering a labyrinth of twisting tunnels and paths leading both up and down through the spire. Using the map they bought from Kentucky Greg, the party was able to set themselves along the path to the armor of the oni king. They began their trek through the abandoned mine they found a gnome named Henly, leading his harem on a scientific expedition through the mines, looking for some sort of special ore. They continued on finding an ancient armory that had a suit or armor that seemed to bring manifest the iconography of the things on the weapons. Solving the riddle in the chamber they found their way to a large dining hall where huge bags of flour fell all around them. Chris Tayshin dispatched the flour with some pyrotechnics and they continued deeper still, finding a large room decorated with dining materials and a hearth burning with magical flame. Nisuki decided to venture further deeper towards a room labeled the store room, where he saw grotesque pale creature climbing on the walls, covered in black eyes. 

They swords killed the gold stealing beast and descended deeper into the mines. After solving a few challenging puzzles, they decided to head for the arch mages quarters where they vanquished the defenders left behind. and laid down for a rest. 

After awakening, the players eventually made their way through the door to the arch mages chambers where they found strange arcane blueprints detailing a the spell forge and its intricate arcane rods that have been inscribed with markings of a spell reminiscent of absorb elements and dispel magic. After clearing through the rest of the floor, the foreman's quarters in particular, where they found war plan showing a red portal deep 2 floors below. They returned to the archmages quarters to lay down for a rest. 

After resting, the swords continued deeper still into the long forgotten mine, the party came across treasures abound, in the form of a large golden golem, which attempted to steal the valuables from the party. Killing the monster, they hid the massive amount of gold in 2 large urns and continued deeper into the mine. Descending to the final bloodstained level of the mine, they found a 4 armed demonic freakish creature that attempted to kill the party. The swords were able to kill the creature and claim the armor of the oni king. 

Bologar had detailed he was leaving in 3 days time from the point in time they left for the copper spire.

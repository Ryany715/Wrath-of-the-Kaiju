## Town Blacksmith

The Chitinous Shell is run by a Dwarven man named Beltharn Bloodfist. Beltharn has lived in Kihoso all his life and primary creates specialty chiton armor from the parts of Kabuto. These items are equivalent to their normal counterparts but give resistance to acid damage. They are 50 percent more expensive than their normal counterparts. In exchange for enough Kabuto parts to create a new piece of heavy chitin armor, he will teach the players how to create the Chitinous armor. 

Beltharn is a representative of the rising blossoms and will ask the party for help if they inquire about the state of the town. He will provide the rising blossoms Adventure hook. 

He will also show the players his schematics for his advanced designs if they ask - these are the nomi specific items.

## Water Caravan

The water caravan will not arrive until the day after the players arrive is Kihoso. 

In the center of town is a caravan arthropod with huge jugs of clean looking water in it. There is a Silk Purse representative there touting "Fresssssh water, come and get your Fressssh water" with a a rather large line leading up to the caravan. 

The silk purse representative is ryokido lion turtle named Odal(brooklyn accent). Odal is selling water on behave of "Turtle Park" a company who boats over water from the city of [[Wrath of the Kaiju/Locations/Puyi/Puyi]] and sells it at a massive markup to the people of Kihoso. Odal details he is looking for someone and will ask they players if they have seen "a black scaled ryujin". He will offer them payment and the adventure hook for the silk purse. People will gladly talk on how the prices of water have gone up since last week, and they feel they will never recover. 

Water price day they arrive: 1 water skin(1 gallon) for 1 gold

## Temple of Golden Rain

Location of the temple well from the module, but also houses the golden armband desired by [[Muchu-Nata]]'s backstory, and is also one of the folklore stories from [[Mirran]]'s backstory. The golden armband was lost by [[Ashura]] long ago, and had since been possessed by a tsukumogami who is able to produce 1 gp a day to someone if it desires. The ring is simply resting in the temple, which is largely used for public events in town. The ring rests on the gift alter in the center of the temple. It has not found "friend" in some time and believes the monks too dull to keep it entertained. When the players first encounter it, it will telepathically communicate with them, wondering if they are worthy of the band. 

In reality the yokai simply wants someone to play with it, as it attempts to hide from them, piloting the band around the temple to various places, if they play along it will invoke the magical power of the band and produce a single gold piece. With some coaxing and convincing, it will even accompany them, believing the temple to be quite the dull place. 

Nystul's magic Aura has been cast on the band to hid its true nature and celestial tie to Ashura. The band's "aura" is one of transmutation magic, the enchantment that produces a single gold each time. In reality this band is an artifact of Ashura, and helps to restore him to his former glory. 

The artifact of Ashura that lies underneath is a specially infused with the essence of Ashura, granting the user increased strength, but cursing them with blood lust. 

The temple well has been supplying the town with the majority of recent water, but it has recently been drying up. 

A monk named George (normal accent) provided the party with information regarding the Golden temple and some of the history of the town. West of the town, about 1 hours walk away. 

## Kihoso Kabuto Hive

About an hour outside of town, Beltharn Bloodfist (london accent) of the Chitinous Shell noticed a very strange behavior of Kabuto building a nest. He notes this to be strange as Kabuto typically don't build nests, but opt for creating a breeding area in the open air around pools of water. He notes as well that some of the male Kabuto in the area have been acting more aggressive. Typically the Kabuto don't bother others as long as the eggs are left alone, the male Kabuto have begun to aggressively charge anyone who gets too close. 

The kabuto hive has been dug up by a male kabuto who is especially large. This male has been digging deeper and deeper trying to get closer to Nomi. The caves have a spiral descending pattern, with other small Kihosan beetles who are following in the wake of the "Alpha male beetle". The larger male has been causing chaos in the area and stirring up the other males, killing him will decrease the aggression of the nearby Kabuto's around Kihoso.

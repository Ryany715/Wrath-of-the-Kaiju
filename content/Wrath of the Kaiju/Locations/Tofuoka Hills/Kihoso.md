## Overview
‘Lumpy’, ‘stinky’, and ‘creepy’ are words commonly used to describe Kihōsō city by those returning from a vacation to the Tofuoka Hills. They’re not wrong; except for the grandiose structures like the Horn Pits and temple-well, buildings in the region are grown from the pod-like Alveolus plants which, from a distance, appear like the stalks of brussels sprouts or bunches of grapes. Moreover, the high sulphur content of the area laces every breeze with a fresh waft of rotting eggs, though most locals say you get used to it after a couple of weeks. As for ‘creepy’ . . . well, if you’re not used to bugs bigger than the palm of your hand, the sight of 10-foot-tall beetles-of-burden might be a little off-putting.

## Districts/Locations

### The Chitinous Shell

The Chitinous Shell is run by a Dwarven man named [[Beltharn Bloodfist]]. Beltharn has lived in Kihoso all his life and primary creates specialty chiton armor from the parts of Kabuto. These items are equivalent to their normal counterparts but give resistance to acid damage. They are 50 percent more expensive than their normal counterparts. In exchange for enough Kabuto parts to create a new piece of heavy chitin armor, he will teach the players how to create the Chitinous armor. 

Beltharn is a representative of the rising blossoms and asked the players to help find a Ryujin named [[Yoshito]]. 
### Kihoso Inn
Local Inn that is in a particularly ornate kihoso, run by a Orc named [[Blurg Blorn]], Blurg is a member of the [[heirs of Kirin]] and he feels deeply for their plight. He tells the players about a visitor named [[Dr. Doolots]] and encourages they seek her out for information on what is going on with the local kabuto. 

2gps buy a room for the day and a gallon of water. 
### Water Caravan

In the center of town is a caravan arthropod with huge jugs of clean looking water in it. There is a Silk Purse representative, [[Odal]] there touting "Fresssssh water, come and get your Fressssh water" with a a rather large line leading up to the caravan. 

Odal is selling water on behave of "Turtle Park" a company who boats over water from the city of [[Wrath of the Kaiju/Locations/Puyi/Puyi]] and sells it at a massive markup to the people of Kihoso. Odal details he is looking for someone and will ask they players if they have seen "a black scaled ryujin". He will offer them payment for finding him and bringing him to Odal. People will gladly talk on how the prices of water have gone up since last week, and they feel they will never recover. 

1 water skin(1 gallon) for 1 gold 

## Surrounding Wilderness Locales

### Kihoso Kabuto Hive
About an hour outside of town, [[Beltharn Bloodfist]] of the Chitinous Shell noticed a very strange behavior of Kabuto building a nest. He notes this to be strange as Kabuto typically don't build nests, but opt for creating a breeding area in the open air around pools of water. He notes as well that some of the male Kabuto in the area have been acting more aggressive. Typically the Kabuto don't bother others as long as the eggs are left alone, the male Kabuto have begun to aggressively charge anyone who gets too close. 

The kabuto hive has been dug up by a male kabuto who is especially large. This male has been digging deeper and deeper trying to get closer to Nomi. The caves have a spiral descending pattern, with other small Kihosan beetles who are following in the wake of the "Alpha male beetle". The larger male has been causing chaos in the area and stirring up the other males, killing him will decrease the aggression of the nearby Kabuto's around Kihoso.

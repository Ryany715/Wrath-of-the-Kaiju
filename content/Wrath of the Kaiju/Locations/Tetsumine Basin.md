## Ancient Copper Spire -  Copper Number 13 

The spirefolk of old sought to mine out the copper of the spire during the bronze age of Shen Ji. When smelted together with tin, the ore formed a strong and plentiful metal that was used by the kingdom to fuel the technological developments of the realm. Copper spire #13 was found towards the beginning of the bronze age, and was found to be quite special. Deep within the spire, a thin connection between the elemental realm of fire has caused latent magics to leak through and cause strange arcano metallic manifestations in the metals. In particular they found that the copper, when resonating, formed an anti magical barrier in a radius around it. This prompted high demand for the copper from this spire , as it can be infused into other metallic implements in order to create magically nullifying implements( bells, shields, necklaces).

The spirefolk of old began to construct spell forges in this spire, large arcanoforges where they attempted to extract the magical properties of the arcanometals they were farming, attempting to create new spells, and place the properties of the metal into other objects. 

The spirefolk of old prioritized this spire and dug deep and fast, excavating this spire in its entirety. The deep they dug, the thinner the veil between the plane of fire and the prime material became, eventually discovering a rift deep beneath the spire itself. Through this rift came the incursion of the red oni, wrathful angry oni from the realm of fire, who sought to break into the prime material and form clans of their own on the prime material plane.

##### Arch mage quarters

The archmage of the spire and the fight against the oni king was name Malgi Copperbeard. Magli was a dwarf of old who was obsessed with the idea of infusing metals with the power of magic. He envisioned a world where even those without innate magical ability could access magics by carrying magically imbued equipment with them that could cast all manner of magics. He saw a world where magic helps to equalize and help others, not drive a divide between the magical and non magical creatures. 
His quarters are a deluge of long forgotten projects, tinkering materials and ingots of copper strewn about some pulsing with magical energies. 

* arcana check 15 + will reveal these copper ingots have been painstakingly carved with magical runes across the surface. The copper seems to resonate with magics in some of the ingots. *
His quarters contain a bed and large workbench area that contains all of these experiments. Additionally there is a locked chest at the base of his bed. In the chest are his notes. Of note from these are the following passages, written in drawvish: 

1. Suspicions are correct. Metals pulled from this spire seem to be more receptive to infusion, likely due to close proximity to elemental plane thinning. These will make excellent candidates for the creation of various arcano-metal experiments. Copper is not the most ideal metal for weapon and armor purposes, but will have to do for now.
2. Its proving difficult to allow the metal to retain magical properties after infusion. Runic engraving process is manual and painstaking, requiring many hours for a single ingot. Furthermore, working the ingot causes the symbology to break and significantly weakens or destroys the magical enchantment. The problem lies in infusion of magics into the very being of the metal
3. Construction of the spell forge began today. The large tuning rods should act as sufficient spellcasting focuses to infuse during the forging process. This will allow for infusion during the forging of the ingot, hopefully allowing for magical infusion into the molten metal, leading to stability later on during the manufacturing process
4. Spell forge construction has completed, and the first test was quite strenuous. Initial technique of simply "shotgun blasting" enchantment magics into the motel metal had some success. The product is crude however, not allowing for granular fine tuning of the spell parameters and serves no real additional benefit over simply enchanting an item in the first place(minus material cost).
5. It came in a dream last night. the lightbulb moment if you will. It would be nigh impossible to etch runes of enchantment into the surface of molten metals, and finetuning the enchantment process would lead to a delicacy that is near impossible with the existing tuning rods present. I have been thinking of the problem all wrong, the magical potential of the metals is not meant to be infused during the forging process, but rather enhanced. One must consider these metals as a sponge, ready to accept magics imparted onto them, because they themselves have some amount of magical propensity. Imagine instead we prime these sponges but extracting as much water as we can from them, removing the magical properties already present, creating a void, a dried sponge ready to suck magics up like water.
6. EUREKA. It worked. The tuning rods have been repurposed as extraction rods. The new spell is working splendidly. It took months to prepare, but the magic functions as intended a bit of permutation of the 2 spells and has created, what I will call Magli's Mootification... hmmm. Name is WIP.

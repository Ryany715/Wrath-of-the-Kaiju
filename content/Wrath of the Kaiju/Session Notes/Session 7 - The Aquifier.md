## Session Overview

The party encountered Nomi, the Quaking Earth — a massive kabuto kaiju who had been polluting Kihoso's water supply with potent acid while nesting in and purifying the aquifer. Nomi had been laying her eggs in the aquifer, acidifying the reservoir. She burrowed toward the surface with the party in pursuit, breaching near the Temple of the Golden Rain and beginning to acidify its water supply. The party engaged the kaiju in a brutal battle, losing Kofra Esleufan to Nomi's claws, before finally bringing the beast down with a massive combined attack.

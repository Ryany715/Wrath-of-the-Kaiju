## Session Overview

The Swords of Kihoso were given the morning to settle their affairs before setting off to sea aboard the Fogrunner. Muchu rushed back into Puyi to meet with Chuji's mother, paying a large sum of gold to have empty crates placed around the city. The party finished their preparations, did some strange cleaning aboard the ship, and settled in for the voyage ahead. The captain detailed the plan for the coming ghost pirate raid, warning the party to expect boarders during the defense of the supply lines. The next morning, the Fogrunner sailed into the oncoming onslaught — and was the only ship to survive the assault.

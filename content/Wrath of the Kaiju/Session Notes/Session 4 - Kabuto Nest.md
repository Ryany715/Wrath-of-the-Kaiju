## Session Overview

The party came across the 2 large Kabuto beetles guarding the nest deep to the north of Kihoso. After scouting, they killed the guardian protecting the eggs and pressed onward, discovering a large kabuto beetle digging deeper into the ground — the same bug responsible for the nest. After a challenging battle, they slew the beetle and towed its corpse back to Kihoso, arriving to a crowd of onlookers.

## Key Events & Discoveries

### Scene 1: The Kabuto Nest
*   The party scouted the nest and decided to kill the bug guarding the eggs before proceeding further into the cave.
*   Deeper in, they discovered a large kabuto beetle digging a hole further into the ground — the same beetle responsible for digging out the nest.

### Scene 2: The Battle & Return to Kihoso
*   The party fought a challenging battle against the large digging kabuto and emerged victorious.
*   They towed the beetle's corpse back to Kihoso, arriving at the town's edge to stares and pointing from townsfolk.
*   Beltharn declined to pay for the corpse but advised bringing it to Dr. Doolots instead.
*   Dr. Doolots noted the beetle's hormone gland was engorged — a physiological change typical of bugs in heat or attempting to mate.

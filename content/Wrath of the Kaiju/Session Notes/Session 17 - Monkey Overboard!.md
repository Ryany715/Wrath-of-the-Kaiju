## Recap from Last Session
*   The Fogrunner sailed into an ambush by ghost pirates, the only ship in its convoy to survive.

## Session Overview

The Swords of Kihoso successfully fended off the ghostly pirate attack and regrouped with Captain Ironfin and the crew of the Fogrunner. They spent the night making repairs before setting out the next morning to search the wreckage of the battle for survivors. They found none, but did recover several crates, which they reported back to the ship. Asulei was approached by Diloophi with a proposition to meet later that night. After helping with repairs, the party met Diloophi in a dinghy off the side of the ship, where he explained his plan to use Stillbreath anemone extract to put the captains to sleep and steal the ship — mentioning as well that he'd heard rumors of Mistraider privateers in the area. The party deliberated the merits of his plan and ultimately ended the conversation with Muchu placing a hand on Diloophi's shoulder and pushing him overboard — he can't swim.

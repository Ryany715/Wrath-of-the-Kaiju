## Recap from Last Session
*   Captain Sawtooth offered the party a contract to investigate the ghost pirates plaguing the Puyi–Umigiri Coast trade routes.
*   Muchu learned that what he's seeking lies hidden within the college spire of metallurgy.

## Session Overview

The party decided to take Captain Sawtooth's contract and set out to find magical equipment for their new ally, Asulei. Venturing into the Guts for the first time, they made for the Rat's Respite but were stopped by a group of robed thugs demanding an entrance fee. The party refused, killed the thugs, and threw their bodies from the rafters above the Guts' streets — one crashing into the crowd below and crushing several bystanders. They then took Captain Sawtooth up on his offer, boarding the Fogrunner, a ship escorting the supply line convoys. On the way out, the party briefly crossed paths with the Oracle, a man bearing the same insignia as the thugs they'd just killed. Once aboard, they were welcomed by Diloophi and brought to Captain Ironfin's quarters.

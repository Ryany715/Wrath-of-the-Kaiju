"I was born into the Blue Blaze. A nomadic people’s group who lived mostly off of the
land. Occasionally we send our most respected into nearby towns to trade, and gather
information. We sought tales of powerful beasts and Yokai that endangered others. If we caught
wind of such creatures, we tracked them down and sent our warriors to remove the threat, and
our craftspeople turned their corpses into weapons, armor, clothes, and potions. The best kept
within the troop, while some were provided as trading goods or gifts for peaceful travel through
others lands. I was an only child, but it didn’t feel that way in the troop. All the children were
cared for by everyone. As I grew older, I grew stronger, and it was clear that my path was to
become one of the warriors of the group. I trained and trained and upon my 15th birthday I
joined in my first fight. After I proved myself against a swarm of giant centipedes, I was accepted
into our vanguard, fighting alongside my father and my tribal brothers and sisters. We always
had occasional injuries, and a few loses here and there against more threatening opponents, but
the troop remained strong. That was until a few years ago, just after I had reach 32 years of age.
We had heard rumors of Kaiju sightings in a nearby town, a new age of turmoil had begun. It
was told in our troops history that during the previous age of Kaiju we had tracked kaiju and
followed in their footsteps. Not because we stood a chance in defeating them, however chaos
also seemed to follow. Escalations of the local fauna, beasts and yokai seemed to be enraged in
their wake. And we would be there to slay them, to stop them, to hunt them. While this time
would be the most dangerous for our troop, its also our truest, what we were meant to do. But
this time it was different. The kaiju we were following seemed to vanish. We couldn't find any
trace of it. And when we began to camp and spread out to pick up signs of it, it was too late.
Was it an ambush, did we miss something, or was it sheer randomness, I’ll never know. But
before we knew it the Kaiju was upon us, and it seems we did something to incur its wrath. I
grabbed my Kanabo and charged into the fray, doing my best to try and defend those who
couldn't defend themselves. But what chance did I have? I was smashed against the ground and
flung across the camp. I tumbled down a nearby hill into the forest below. As my pain and
injuries got the better of me, I lost consciousness as I heard the screams of my troop being
slaughtered. I thought I died, but eventually I opened my eyes once more. And before me a
vision of the most beautiful woman I had and will ever see. She told me heard the commotion
and when she got closer to look she saw me tumble down into the forest beside her. She
brought me to her hideout in the woods and brought me back from the brink. I couldn’t believe it,
my people were gone and this woman saved me? I wanted to go back, to see for myself, but my
wounds were too severe. I was stuck here. I swore revenge on the kaiju, but I thought I would
never recover to the level to pick up a weapon again, let alone travel and fight. But this woman,
she healed me, more than what should be possible. As we talked and grew closer, she revealed
that she was no normal woman, but a yokai. She used her powers to repair me, to make me
strong again. After a few months, I was strong enough to leave, but I had grown fond of her
company. I asked if she wished to travel with me, to find others that could help me slay this Kaiju and protect others from the fate my troop endured. She agreed, and we traveled the realm for
the next couple of years. However, she revealed to me that she too had been wounded. She
wasn’t sure by what, a curse, an illness, the imbalance of the world? Something was taking a
toll, and with each passing moment she grew ever so slightly weaker. I did my best to provide for
us as we traveled, hunting down small beasts, trading the meats and pelts for food, lodging and
medicines. They seemed to help her condition, but just for a short while, and before long she
grew even weaker. I vowed that just as she cared for me, I would do the same for her. Maybe it
was our situations, maybe it was the time spent on the road, just the two of us. But as we grew
closer together, a fondness for each other deepened. And we fell in love. But my love continued
to wither before me. Soon she would be too weak to travel. We eventually stumbled across a
shrine to yokai deep in the forest outside a very small town. An aasimar monk, the only holy
person at the shrine, took pity on us. Alleia said that she would look after (yokai love) and do her
best to steady her health, while I trekked on, looking for stronger remedies. I took one final look
at my love, and promised her I would return, to bring her something that would fix her ailment,
and that eventually we would travel the world together again. Just the two of us. So now I set
forth seeking a band of adventurers who wish to slay stronger and more powerful creatures
eventually Kaiju so that I can gather even more powerful ingredients for remedies for my love.
And, if we can protect innocents along the way, even better."

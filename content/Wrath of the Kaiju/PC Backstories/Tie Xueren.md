"Tie Xueren comes from the Kitakami mountains and has begun his journey after his tribe came under threat by a large metallic, rusted iron Wyvern. During the beasts first appearance it was wounded, though not gravely, and it departed after seemingly realizing we would not be made easy meals of. Tie has now descended from his mountainous homestead in search of the knowledge and means to build potent defenses for his people so that they can be ready should the sky kaiju return. He carries the time honored crafting traditions of his Enkoh tribe and has taken up Lu Ban [big orange orangutan in the sky] as a patron deity to guide his hands."

Tie Xueren's quest to craft comes with some fun additions. For each time he successfully completes a new unique magical craft, his proficiency bonus associated with crafting from scratch goes up(harvesting, manufacturing and enchanting). Ultimately would like to craft a skypiecer type arrow that allows for the slaying of the [[Shen Ji#Iron Wyrm|Iron Wrym]] kaiju that attacked his town.

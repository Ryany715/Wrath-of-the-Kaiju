# History of Shen Ji

Shen Ji is a rich and prosperous lands, home to all manner of beast, creature and spirit. The realm have been shrouded in a mist that has cut them off from the surrounding planes for a millennium, if not more. The traditional gods and goddesses of the prime material and beyond can still communicate with Shen Ji, but anything short of deific magics cannot permeate the ever present mist that surrounds the realm. 

Despite this "seclusion" the beings of this place thrive, and have no desire to leave. The realms also harbors a unique ecosystem, one where the spirts of this realm, the Yokai, are present in all walks of life. Even more strange then the yokai though, are the [[#Kaiju]], colossal forces of nature that shape the very land they walk upon. 

It has been 1000 years since the [[Lei Shen]], and Shen Ji has known relative peace. A time during which the realm has prospered, allowing for the blossoming of society and order. It seems that time of peace and prosperity may be threatened, as in the past few years, the ground itself has begun to sunder, the mountains have begun to move, the storms have begun to rage. Kaiju have been spotted.
# [[Kaiju]]

Kaiju are gargantuan creatures that inhabit Shen Ji. These forces of nature take many forms, massive kabuto beetles, bipedal Raiju, or leviathans made of spiritual water, but share one common trait: their existence shapes the very realm itself. These creatures can cleave mountains with a swipe of a tail, flood a valley with wanton breath, and level entire towns when lying down to rest. Each kaiju is motivated by it's own personal agenda, these creatures are not controlled or manipulated for some higher purpose, they simply exist as sheer power incarnate. 

Kaiju cannot truly be slain, no more so than a storm can be "killed". The death of a kaiju signals the beginning of a cycle of rebirth, some thousands of years in the future, it will be reborn again, ready to wreck havoc once more.

The kaiju who attacked the Blue Blaze in [[Muchu-Nata]]'s backstory. This kaiju was exercising its intense territorial nature in preparation for mating season. It attacked anything that broke through its pheromone boundary, and was preparing it's nest for a mating dance. 

Cloaked killer who uses camouflage tactics to ambush others with swift and lethal force. It is highly territorial and will attack anything that breaks through its pheromone boundary.

A Kaiju of direct opposition to the Raiju, the Iron Wyrm's are at odds with the lightening wielding tigers.

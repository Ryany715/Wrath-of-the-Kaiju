The last great emperor of Shen Ji, history remembers Lei Shen as the great kaiju slayer and bringer of a time of peace. History tends to forget that Lei Shen was a ruthless leader, bringing Shen Ji under the banner of the Thunder King, one of strict law and code. It was said that Lei Shen commanded the power of the storm itself, able summon and bend the power of lightning, and with this power, he was able to defeat the kaiju of the past. 

Lei Shen was born to a royal family of oniborne. The strongest of his siblings he was born to be great, and great he was. Lei Shen aspired for much more than just dominion over his nomadic troop. He saw the power of his people, the power of his claim, and decided he would unite all of Shen Ji under his banner. He sought to tame and destroy the kaiju that ravaged the land, seeing a future where the denizens of Shen Ji could be the rulers of the land, not the kaiju. In the years that followed Lei Shen led a conquest across the lands, uniting the races and tribes under his banner through all means of conquest. For those that could be reasoned with, Lei Shen offered a vision of a Shen Ji ruled by order and prosperity under a single banner. For those who could not, Lei Shen split the heavens with his righteous [[#Power of the Storm]] and made those who opposed him bend the knee. 10 years of conquest, and Lei Shen began the [[#Reign of the Thunder King]]. 
## Power of the Storm

Lei Shen stole a tempest bead from a Raiju while it slumbered. He then had a powerful shaman fuse the bead with himself, granting him great powers of thunder and lightening. He then used the very power of the kaiju to slay them and bring about the years of peace the realm knows now. 
## Reign of the Thunder King

At the start of his reign, the Thunder King led [[#The Hunt of Thunder]] to cleanse the kaiju from the realm. With Shen Ji's people free from the immense force of these behemoths, the emperor could begin to build his empire.

Lei Shen ruled Shen Ji through strength and wit. He united the people of the land under a single unified language (common). He created a single system of weight and currency that laid the foundation for society today. His reign saw the strict enforcement of law and order, with insurrection and rebellion punished by public evisceration and execution. Any and all who opposed his rule were subjected and enslaved. 

He erected the walled city of [[Puyi]], the capital of his empire which lied in the center of the [[Puyi#Thunder Plains|Thunder Plains]].

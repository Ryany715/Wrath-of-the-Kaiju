## Background
Yoshito was a kabuto trainer at Kihoso's horn pits who went missing, first mentioned by Beltharn Bloodfist and later investigated by the party at the horn pits. The party eventually found him alive at the aquifer beneath Kihoso, alongside his kabuto "The Decapitator," fixated on a massive hatched egg — presumably tied to Nomi's brood. The party watched as Nomi appeared and cleaved Yoshito in 2 beneath the city. 

He was later lifted up by the swords of Kihoso as the founder and hero of the group and the city ( he was not)
## Motivations & Goals
To train "The Decaptiator" to be the best fighting Kabuto around

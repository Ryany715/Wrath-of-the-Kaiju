## Background
Odal leads the water caravan that supplies Kihoso. Knowing the temple well had been drying up, he arrived a day earlier than expected to sell his water at 1 gp per gallon, drawing a growing line in the town center.

## Motivations & Goals
To profit from Kihoso's worsening water shortage by controlling the caravan trade.

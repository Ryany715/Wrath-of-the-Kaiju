# Dean Carbone

*   **Personality:** Ambitious and research-driven.

## Background
Dean Carbone was overheard by Nisuke and Chris Tayshin discussing succession planning with Dean Bombuku ("he should choose his successor based on merit") at the College of Hanabi. The two deans detailed their work on a self-healing material and offered to pay Nisuke for help acquiring it from a kaiju that has arisen in the Pondermoss Weald to the north. Carbone also mentioned extensive research, built off the work of an expelled adjunct faculty member, that could fuse two entities into one.

## Motivations & Goals
To advance his research — particularly the self-healing material project and the entity-fusion research.

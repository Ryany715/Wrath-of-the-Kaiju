# Chuji Firestorm

## Description
*   **Appearance:** 
![[Wrath of the Kaiju/NPCs/Pictures/Chuji Firestorm.png]]
*   **Personality:** The epitome of charisma, Chūji is a natural leader, able to charm young and old alike. Though they don the airs of the high and mighty, they act generously towards the common folk.
*   **Mannerisms:** Chūji has an easy way with words, using the accent and slang of the noble youths to crack jokes and put people at ease

## Background
Chuji was originally met outside the gates of the ringlander town in the Shinihai Flats. Chuji approached the party at the Shinihai Flats to buy the broken Raiju crossbow they found ascending the Ironspire; they kept the weapon but let her study its construction. The party later overheard [[Nishikibe]] attempting to buy her out of a contract, and confirmed through interrogation that she is part of the Rising Blossom organization. After the defunct deal, [[Nishikibe]] offered a huge bounty on her head to Nisuke. When Nisuke instead warned Nishikibe of the deal, Chuji suggested they consider "gutting Nishikibe" themselves.

Chuji informed the party that her family in Puyi is wealthy and well connected, the Firestorm family that runs the The Standardized Spell Weaver.

## Motivations & Goals
To stoke the fires of rebellion and destroy the Silk Purse and their tyrannical control over the realm and the city of Puyi.

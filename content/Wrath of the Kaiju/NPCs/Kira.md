# Kira

## Description
*   **Appearance:** A beautiful young woman. A disturbing spider creature.
![[Kira.png]]
*   **Personality:** Warm and welcoming on the surface, concealing a manipulative and dangerous true nature — capable of charming and commanding others as loyal "allies."
*   **Mannerisms:** 

## Background
Kira tended the graveyard at the base of the Tree of Life and welcomed the Swords of Kihoso in warmly, along with her other guests ([[Dr. Frank]] and [[Mon]]), offering a hearty meal and fireside tales. That night, she lured Muchu into the basement beneath her home. When he broke free of her hold and set fire to the door, shooting his way out, the rest of the party rushed to investigate. A pitched battle followed, with Kira commanding charmed "allies" against the party — ending in her death and the death of Crabagail, one of her guests, who was executed by Muchu while under her charm.

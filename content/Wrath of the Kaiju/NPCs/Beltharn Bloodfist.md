# Beltharn Bloodfist

## Description
*   **Appearance:** 
*   **Personality:** Practical and business-minded — offered the party a bounty and taught them his masterwork crafting recipes
*   **Mannerisms:** Vaguely British

## Background
Beltharn is the town blacksmith of Kihoso. He first told the party about the strange, aggressive behavior of the kabuto beetles building a nest north of the city, offering up to 150 gold pieces to clear it out, along with knowledge of his masterwork crafting recipes (chitin armor pricing: whole chitin 25–100 gp worth of whole → plate, 50gp + 50gp others → splint; medium pieces 17 gp, breastplate 50 gp; small pieces 3 gp, studded 10 gp). He also first mentioned that a black ryujin kabuto trainer named [[Yoshito]] had gone missing from the horn pits.

When the party later returned with the corpse of the large kabuto from the nest, Beltharn declined to pay for it, redirecting them to [[Dr. Doolots]] instead — the corpse's engorged hormone gland was outside his expertise.

## Motivations & Goals
Had plans for master worked Kabuto armor and weapons.

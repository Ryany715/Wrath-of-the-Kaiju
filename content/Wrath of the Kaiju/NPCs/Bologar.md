## Background
Bologar met the party in the Shinhai flats, and detailed that he was heading to the Ironspire to seek out Raiko, a kaiju he believes is causing the region's storm. He sought to face Raiko in an epic duel to prove his power and martial might. He also sought to help the monks who had been lost there. 
He led the party's ascent up the mountain to the Ironspire, guiding them through storm elemental attacks and treacherous ravines, and ultimately to the confrontation with Raiko. During the confrontation he chased the monks who seemingly summoned the kaiju, and killed as many as he could in the tunnels that lead deeper into the Ironspire. He returned to the fight just in time to aid the party and ground Raiko into the earth by pushing a massive statue of an Iron Wrym onto his dying form. 

## Motivations & Goals
To prove his martial might and honor are resolute. To find stronger and stronger foes to best.

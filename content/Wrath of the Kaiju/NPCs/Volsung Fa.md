## Background
Volsung Fa is a roaming warlord not yet met directly by the party. Captain Geo warned them of him while they crossed the Thunder Plains — not much is known about Fa, but the city of Puyi fears his might, and his efforts to unite the local tribes are considered dangerous.

## Motivations & Goals

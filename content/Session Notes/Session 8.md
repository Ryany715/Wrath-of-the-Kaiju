## Recap from Last Session
*   The party defeated Nomi, the Quaking Earth, at the Temple of the Golden Rain
*   Nomi's corpse now lies at the party's feet.

## Session Overview

The players harvested what they could from Nomi's corpse before the temple began to be overrun with kabuto beetles swarming toward a mounting cloud of acid. Scrambling back to town, they brought pieces of Nomi's corpse to Ryoko, who used chronomancy to reveal the beast's past as a hunter of kaiju. Muchu secretly consulted his new talking artifact, Baki. Blurg Blorn, representing the heirs of Kirin, rewarded the party with 3000 gold pieces on behalf of the town, and Dr. Doolots briefed them on rumors from the Tetsumine Basin.

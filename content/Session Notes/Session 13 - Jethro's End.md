## Recap from Last Session
*   Nishikibe murdered Kofra in cold blood at the Shinihai Flats.
*   The party fled town, heading toward Puyi, roughly a week's travel away.

## Session Overview

The Swords of Kihoso traveled across the Thunder Plains toward the city of Puyi. Along the way they evaded a nomadic warrior party under cover of night and were warned by Captain Geo of the Pegasus Guard about the warlord Volsung Fa, as well as two dangerous points of interest: the Evernight Gate and Thunderbird Point. Mirran's egg hatched a creature named Jethro, who was immediately killed. Arriving in Puyi, the party visited Mildred's magic shop in the Moor of Merchants, acquiring gear and learning of a flying carpet available for gold or a Thunderbird feather. The party then split up to pursue separate leads at the Dockyards and the College of Hanabi, where Nisuke and Chris Tayshin learned of Dean Bombuku's and Dean Carbone's ongoing research, including a mysterious project capable of fusing two entities into one.

## Key Events & Discoveries

### Scene 1: Crossing the Thunder Plains
*   The Swords of Kihoso traveled across the [[Puyi#Thunder Plains]], about a week's journey to the city of Puyi.
![[Thunder Plains.png]]
*   [[PC - Muchu]] led the party; they encountered a large nomadic warrior party in the early hours of the night and used the cover of darkness to pass unnoticed.
*   The next day, the party met members of the [[Puyi#Pegasus Guard]], specifically Captain Geo, 7th Plume of the 7th Battalion of the Guard.
*   Geo warned the party of a roaming warlord named Volsung Fa, a massive orc in tank-like, iron-spiked armor, whose uniting of the tribes concerns the city.
*   Geo also mentioned two notable locations: the Evernight Gate, where mangled traveler remains have been found, and Thunderbird Point, home to massive elemental birds a few miles outside town.

### Scene 2: Jethro's Hatching
*   That night, Jethro hatched from Mirran's egg, revealing a stingray-esque, skin-flapped creature.
*   Jethro was killed in cold blood, leaving [[PC - Mirran]] in shock.
*   ![[Jethro.png]]

### Scene 3: Arrival in Puyi & Mildred's Shop
*   The party continued into the [[City of Puyi]].
![[City of Puyi.jpg]]
*   They visited the Moor of Merchants to buy magical goods from Mildred's shop.
*   The party acquired plate armor, a set of +1 daggers, and a +1 lance.
*   Mildred offered to craft a flying carpet for 20,000 gold, or 3,000 gold if the party could acquire a feather from a willing Thunderbird.

### Scene 4: The Party Splits
*   [[PC - Muchu]] went to ask Mildred if she could scry on an object he is searching for.
*   [[PC - Dr. Frank]], [[PC - Mon]], and [[PC - Mirran]] headed to the Dockyards, specifically a tavern called the Barnacle.
*   [[PC - Nisuke]] and [[PC - Chris Tayshin]] headed to the College of Hanabi for a reunion.

### Scene 5: The Barnacle
*   At the Barnacle, the group encountered Captain Whitebeard, the establishment's proprietor.
*   A middle-aged man approached the group and offered to buy them a drink given their growing fame.
*   As the scene cut away, a frazzled sailor stumbled in yelling about "those damn ghosts" attacking again.

### Scene 6: The College of Hanabi
*   Nisuke and Chris Tayshin overheard a conversation between Dean Bombuku and Dean Carbone, discussing that "he should choose his successor based on merit."
*   The pair met with the two academics, who detailed their work on a self-healing material and offered to pay Nisuke for help acquiring it from a kaiju that has arisen in the Pondermoss Weald to the north.
*   Dean Carbone mentioned extensive research, built off the work of an expelled adjunct faculty member, that could fuse two entities into one.

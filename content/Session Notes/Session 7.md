The player encountered Nomi, The quaking earth, a massive kabuto kaiju who had been polluting the water supply of Kihoso with potent acid deep underneath the city while purifying the aquifer. Nomi turned and began to burrow towards the surface with the party in hot pursuit, eventually breaching to the surface. The beetle barreled its way to the temple of the golden rain, where it began to acidify the temple water supply. The party decided it must be stopped, and confronted the beast, just barely managing to make it out alive. The corpse of the monstrosity now lies at the feet of the kaiju slayers.

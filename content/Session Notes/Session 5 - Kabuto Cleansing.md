## Recap from Last Session
*   The party slew the large kabuto beetle responsible for digging the nest and towed its corpse back to Kihoso.
*   Dr. Doolots noted the beetle's engorged hormone gland, suggesting mating-related behavior.
*   Bombuku confirmed the nest's acid sample still wasn't strong enough for his purposes.

## Session Overview

The players arrived back in town with the kabuto corpse and briefly split up, with Muchu and Kofra heading to the Golden Rain Temple while the others explored town. Regrouping, they spent time in the horn pits gathering information on the missing Yoshito, uncovering ties between the horn pits and the Silk Purse. The party then descended into the tunnels beneath the city toward the aquifer, encountering a group of deep gnomes led by Joob, who took interest in Muchu's gun and gave them a treasure map, along with word of the deep gnome city of Blingdenstone.

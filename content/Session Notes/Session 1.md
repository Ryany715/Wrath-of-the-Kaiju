## Session Overview

The players converged on the nexus of the path westward towards the city of Kihoso. With some introductions made amongst new traveling companions they boarded the "bug bus" a large Rhinoceros beetle with a carriage affixed to its back. They boarded the beetle, with Mirran smearing her pheromones onto the bugs head, and it began to travel along the road to the west. Along they way they came across their first yokai encounter, a Kasha attempting to steal the corpse of a monk of the [[Fists of Ashara]]. Killing the Kasha and helping the monks, they invited the party in for some tea. The party learned that the water in the town was slightly acidic and advised the town to move eastward to save themselves as they believed it may only get worse. They told the party as well that the kabuto beetles in the area had been acting strangely aggressive as well  as exhibiting some strange behavior. Continuing on the players came across 2 a smaller kabuto fighting alongside the road, engaging in a wrestling match, with one emerging victorious in pinning the other, causing it to walk away with its head held in defeat. Upon reaching the end of the road, the party arrived at a large tori gate that signals entry into the city of Kihoso.

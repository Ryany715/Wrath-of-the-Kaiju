## Recap from Last Session
*   The party met Dr. Doolots at the horn pits and agreed to help her neutralize the acidic aquifer.
*   Beltharn Bloodfist offered a bounty to clear an aggressive kabuto nest north of the city.
*   The party returned to the Kihoso inn to rest with Bombuku.

## Session Overview

Bombuku gave the players his basalt bottle, asking for a sample straight from the source of the aquifer, as the acidic water in town is still too weak for his purposes. Meanwhile, the water caravan led by Odal arrived a day early to upsell water at 1 gp per gallon, drawing a line in the town center. The party spoke with Dr. Doolots, who explained the descent to the aquifer would take an indeterminate amount of time, and headed off to deal with the kabuto nest north of town, fighting through beetle swarms into a chamber filled with purple mushrooms, a poison puddle, and two large Kabuto guardians.

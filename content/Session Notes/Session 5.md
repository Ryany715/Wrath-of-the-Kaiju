The players arrived back in town with the corpse of the large kabuto beetle they had found in the nest north of Kihoso. The party briefly split, with Muchu and Kofra heading to the golden rain temple and the others spreading out around town. The party decided to spend some time in the horn pits again after meeting back up, trying to gather additional information on Yoshito, the black Ryokidon who had gone missing. They were not able to glean any smoking gun information, but did uncover some intriguing ties of the horn pits to the Silk Purse. The party decided to it was time to descend into the tunnels below the city and make their way to the aquifer. Along the way, they encountered a group of deep gnomes, helmed by a male deep gnome named Joob. Joob took interest in Muchu's gun and a brief back and forth ensued as they ingratiated themselves to one another. Joob eventually gave them a treasure map he said pointed to some treasure top side. Joob also told them of a deep gnome city that lies beneath the surface about a days travel away called Blingdenstone.

## Session Overview

The players continued their descent toward the aquifer beneath Kihoso. They passed a room of strange rock creatures huddled around some kind of project, then found a shallow river leading deeper underground. They encountered and bartered with 2 kappa, dispatched some Kabuto, and reached the shoreline of the massive aquifer. There, they found Yoshito with his kabuto "The Decapitator," fixated on a massive hatched egg. Dr. Doolots explained her device would need about 12 hours to take full effect in these waters, and the party bedded down for the night deep below the surface.

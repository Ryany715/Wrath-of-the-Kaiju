## Recap from Last Session
*   The Swords of Kihoso survived a deadly encounter with Kira at the Tree of Life, losing Crabagail in the process.
*   The party reached the edge of the Tetsumine Basin, overlooking the storm at its center.

## Session Overview

The Swords of Kihoso arrived in the ringlander town within the Tetsumine Basin, meeting Captain Totsuka, who asked for help investigating monks missing near the Ironspire, and warned of the incoming Rising Blossoms and the Silk Purse. The party purchased a treasure map to Copper Spire #13 from Kentucky Greg and sailed there aboard Bo's Big Boats. Delving deep into the spire's mines, they navigated puzzles, fought a gold-stealing beast and a golden golem, and battled their way to the mine's final level, where they defeated a four-armed demonic creature and claimed the armor of the Oni King.
![[Tetsumine Basin.png]]

## Key Events & Discoveries

### Scene 1: Arrival in the Ringlander Town
*   The party arrived in the ringlander town within the Tetsumine Basin.
*   They met Captain Totsuka, who asked for aid investigating monks missing after sailing for the Ironspire.
![[Session Notes/Pictures/Captain Totsuka.png]]
*   Totsuka warned the party of the incoming Rising Blossoms and the Silk Purse.
*   The party sought the patronage of Kentucky Greg, haggling over the price of a treasure map to Copper Spire #13.
*   They boarded Bo's Big Boats and set out for the Copper Spire.

### Scene 2: Into the Copper Spire
*   Bo dropped the party at the Copper Spire, promising to return when summoned via a sending stone.
*   The party discovered a labyrinth of twisting tunnels leading both up and down through the spire.
*   Using Kentucky Greg's map, they set a course toward the armor of the Oni King.
*   They met a gnome named Henly, leading his harem on a scientific expedition in search of a special ore.
*   They found an ancient armory whose armor seemed to manifest the iconography of nearby weapons.
*   Solving a riddle led them to a large dining hall, where huge bags of flour fell around them; [[PC - Chris Tayshin]] dispatched the flour with pyrotechnics.

### Scene 3: The Storeroom Beast & Deeper Mines
*   The party found a room decorated with dining materials and a hearth burning with magical flame.
*   [[PC - Nisuki]] ventured ahead to a room labeled the "store room," finding a grotesque, pale creature covered in black eyes climbing the walls.
![[Dodomeki.png]]
*   The party killed the gold-stealing beast and descended deeper into the mines.
*   After solving further puzzles, they reached and cleared the archmage's quarters before resting.

### Scene 4: The Archmage's Chambers & Foreman's Quarters
*   The party found strange arcane blueprints in the archmage's chambers detailing a spell forge and arcane rods inscribed with markings resembling absorb elements and dispel magic.
![[Iron Spire Forge.jpg]]
*   Clearing the rest of the floor, they found war plans in the foreman's quarters showing a red portal 2 floors below.
*   They returned to the archmage's quarters to rest.

### Scene 5: The Golden Golem & the Oni King's Armor
*   Continuing deeper, the party found abundant treasure, guarded by a large golden golem that attempted to steal their valuables.
*   They killed the golem and hid the gold in 2 large urns before continuing deeper.
*   On the final, bloodstained level of the mine, they encountered a four-armed demonic creature.
*   The party defeated the creature and claimed the armor of the Oni King.
![[Demonic Arms.png]]

## Recap from Last Session
*   The Swords of Kihoso were ambushed by Jorogumo Husks while making camp, resulting in the death of Tie.
*   The party continued their trek toward the Tree of Life on the way to the Tetsumine Basin.

## Session Overview

The Swords of Kihoso dragged Tie's corpse to the Tree of Life, a massive oak spanning a graveyard, where they met a caretaker named Kira and her guests, a doctor and his travelling companion. After a warm meal and fireside tales, Muchu was lured by Kira into the basement beneath her home, where he ultimately turned on her, setting a fire and shooting his way out. A chaotic battle ensued, ending in the deaths of both Kira and Crabagail, with Muchu executing Crabagail under Kira's charm. The party buried the dead, burned the tree and their old belongings behind them, and continued on toward the Tetsumine Basin, meeting a crab person named Chris Tacian along the way before finally reaching the edge of the basin itself.

## Key Events & Discoveries

### Scene 1: Arrival at the Tree of Life & Meeting Kira
*   The party arrived at the [[Tree of Life]], a massive oak spanning a graveyard at its base, dragging Tie's corpse with them.
*   They found the graveyard tended by a beautiful young woman named Kira, who welcomed them warmly.
*   Kira introduced them to her other guests: a doctor and his traveling companion.
*   The party shared a hearty meal and told tales around the fire before retiring for the night.

### Scene 2: Muchu's Encounter with Kira
*   [[PC - Muchu]] chose to sleep outside, where Kira came to fetch him in the night.
*   Kira led him into the basement beneath her home under the tree, asking him to wait.
*   After a time, Muchu steeled himself, realizing he could not be stopped from his current path.
*   He set fire to the interior of the heavy wooden door and began shooting his way out.

### Scene 3: The Battle with Kira
*   The rest of the Swords awoke and headed downstairs to investigate.
*   [[PC - Nisuki]] and [[PC - Mirrin]] initially tried to assure the others that things were fine and they should go back upstairs.
*   Combat with Kira raged on, with her commanding charmed "allies" to prevent the others from leaving.
*   The party was divided in action, eventually leading to the deaths of both Kira and Crabagail.
*   Under Kira's charm, Muchu trained his gun on Crabagail's head and executed him during the fray.

### Scene 4: Aftermath & Continued Travel
*   The Swords of Kihoso buried both Kira and Crabagail.
*   They burned the tree behind them, along with the Nomi chitin and Tie's corpse.
*   Bonds within the party shifted — some strengthening, others weakening — along the road.
*   The party encountered a new Isetsu, a crab person named Chris Tacian.
*   They eventually reached the edge of the [[Tetsumine Basin]], looking out over the rolling storm at its center.

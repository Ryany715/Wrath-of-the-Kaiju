## Recap from Last Session
*   The Swords of Kihoso cleared the Copper Spire, defeating a golden golem and a four-armed demonic creature to claim the armor of the Oni King.
*   Bologar detailed he was leaving in 3 days' time from when the party departed for the spire.

## Session Overview

The party returned to the Shinihai Flats, repairing and upgrading their gear with their newfound wealth, and met again with Captain Totsuka and Bologar, who explained his mission to the Ironspire to confront Raiko, a kaiju he believes responsible for the storm. 
![[Session Notes/Pictures/Bologar.png]]
After a day's delay, the party ascended the mountain toward the Ironspire, weathering storm elementals and a costly mishap with an ancient Wyrmrib crossbow. At the spire's peak, they stole an orb from the monks, provoking a battle with Raiko the Storm Lord, whom they subdued. Returning to the Shinihai Flats, the party was paid by Captain Totsuka, encountered Chuji Firestorm and her ties to the Rising Blossoms, and were drawn into a deadly betrayal that ended with the murder of Kofra by Nishikibe, forcing the party to flee town toward Puyi.

## Key Events & Discoveries

### Scene 1: Return to the Shinihai Flats
*   The party met with Kentucky Greg to repair their gear and purchase new equipment with their spoils.
*   Captain Totsuka reiterated concerns about the missing monks at the Ironspire.
*   Bologar explained his mission to the Ironspire to confront Raiko, a kaiju he believes is causing the ongoing storm.
*   Bologar was convinced to delay departure from morning to afternoon for a "leg day pump."
*   The session ended with cryptic words from [[PC - Muchu]].

### Scene 2: The Ascent to the Ironspire
*   The party spent the morning collecting repaired gear and preparing to depart.
*   Their ascent was met with assaults from storm elementals and treacherous ravines.
*   They found an abandoned outpost partway up the mountain; Dr. Frank attempted to free a massive Wyrmrib crossbow, triggering a trap that caused a massive explosion, partially destroying the weapon.
*   The party reached the spire's precipice and bedded down within the safety of the Ironspire.
*   Late at night, Mon's axe spoke to him, demanding to be fed blood (secretly Baki, pretending to be an evil demon).

### Scene 3: Confronting Raiko
*   The next morning, the party crossed the gorge toward the spire's peak, reading a passage from a book found there.
*   The players stole the orb from the monks, drawing the ire of the storm.
*   They fought Raiko, the Storm Lord, and eventually subdued him beneath the iron wyrm statue atop the monastery.
![[Raiko.png]]

### Scene 4: Return to Shinihai Flats & Rising Tensions
*   The party took Bo's Big Boat back to the Shinihai Flats.
*   Captain Totsuka paid the party on behalf of the heirs of Kirin, and joined them for dinner.
*   Chuji Firestorm approached, asking to buy the broken Raiju crossbow; the party kept it but let her take notes on its construction.
*   The party overheard a blonde-haired businessman attempting to buy Chuji out of a contract; interrogation revealed Chuji is part of the Rising Blossom organization.
*   Chuji declined the buyout offer and later asked Nisuke to kill Chuji (Nishikibe) for 50,000 gold.
*   Nisuke instead warned Chuji about the deal; Chuji suggested gutting Nishikibe themselves.
*   Muchu and Mirran deepened their bond late into the night.
![[Session Notes/Pictures/Chuji Firestorm.png]]
### Scene 5: Betrayal & Kofra's Death
*   The party declined to take either side of the deal; Nisuke chose to sleep in the woods to avoid trouble with Nishikibe.
*   While on watch, Kofra investigated a noise down the hall and "ding-dong ditched" a door.
*   When he returned to knock again, Nishikibe opened the door, struck him down with a cone of cold and shocking grasp combo, dragged him inside, and slit his throat.
*   The rest of the Swords of Kihoso armored up and investigated, finding Kofra's corpse stuffed in a trunk alongside his chest armor.
*   The party de-escalated the situation, retrieved Kofra's body and armor, and fled town toward Puyi, roughly a week's travel away.
![[Session Notes/Pictures/Nishikibe.png]]

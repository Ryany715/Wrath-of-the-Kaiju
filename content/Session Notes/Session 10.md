The swords of kihoso drag the corpse of Tie through the forest to the Tree of Life, a massive oak tree that spans over a graveyard at its base. They found the graveyard was being tended by a beautiful young women named Kira. Kira welcomed them in with open arms, and introduced them to her other guest, a Doctor and his travelling companion.  They had a warm hearty meal, and went off to bed after some tales around the fire. Muchu decided to sleep outside, where Kira came to fetch him in the wee hours of the night. Kira awoke him with her charming presence and led him into the basement below her home, under the tree. He asked him to wait there a while, which he initially complied. After some time, he steeled himself, realizing that he cannot be stopped and slowed down from the path he currently walked. He set fire to the inside of the hefty wooden door and began to shoot his gun in an attempt to escape. The swords awoke, heading downstairs. Initially insistent on investigating the noise, Nisuki and Mirrin began to assure everyone that things are fine and they should just go back upstairs. 

Combat with Kira raged on, her commanding her new "allies" to aid her and not allow the others to leave her cabin. The party found themselves divided in action, eventually leading to the death of both Kira, and Crabagail. The swords of Kihoso decide to bury both Kira and Crabagail in the wake of the battle and continue down the path towards the Tetsumine Basin. Along the way, they began to forge bonds and alliances within the group itself, some strengthening, and others weakening. The Swords continued on until they came across a strange new Isetsu, a crab person named Chris Tacian. Onwards still they eventually made their way to the Tetsumine Basin, which they now sit on the very edge of, looking out towards the rolling storm in the center of the crater.

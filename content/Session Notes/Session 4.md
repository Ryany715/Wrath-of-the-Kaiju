The party came across 2 large Kabuto beetles deep within the nest found to the north of Kihoso. The party scouted ahead some and decided the best decision was to kill the bug guarding their eggs, and then proceed onwards into the cave further. After further investigation, they discovered a large kabuto beetle digging a hole deeper into the ground, seemingly the same bug who dug out the nest in the first place. After a challenging battle with the bug, the party emerged victorious, slaying the big bug  they decided to head back towards Kihoso, towing the bug along with them. They arrived to the precipice of Kihoso with the corpse in tow, with members of the town beginning to point and stare. 

Beltharn will not pay for the corpse but will advise the players to potential bring the corpse to Dr. Dolots in town. The Dr will note that the hormone gland on the bug was engorged, a pyslogial  change that typically when the bugs are in heat or trying to mate. 
 The alpha Kabuto the players fight will have the materials needed for the blacksmith quest upon a successful harvest. They will also find that this small wild Kabuto nest is home to 5 females who are all laying their eggs in a nearby acidified pond of water.

A kitsune tamaya rogue, Nisuke was a common thief who patrolled the streets of [[Wrath of the Kaiju/Locations/Puyi/Puyi]] looking for the next big score to fill his pockets with gold. After overhearing tell of a fantastic new arcanomech technology developed by the [[College of Hanabi]], Nisuki couldn't help but try his hand at "acquiring" some of the tech for himself. She underestimated the savviness one Professor Bombuku, who caught him red handed in the act. Bombuku saw opportunity and promise in the entrepreneurial kitsune, and struck up a deal with him. In exchange for his help and services, he wouldn't report him to the authorities, and would even provide her with a steady employment and stream of income. He enlisted his help in procuring some of the more hard to find materials for his research, employing her to use whatever methods she saw fit for acquisition. He even saw it as an opportunity to field test some of his newer inventions! 

Until recently, Bombuku has not sent Nisuki on any missions too far outside the city of [[Wrath of the Kaiju/Locations/Puyi/Puyi]], but today is different. Bombuku has asked Nisuke to "We leave in the morning, west to the city of [[Wrath of the Kaiju/Locations/Tofuoka Hills/Kihoso]], there are reports of some strange ecological shifts, tremors in the ground. I have heard the acidic properties of the area have increased in concentration and believe it to be the perfect catalyst for increased capabilities in the arcane etching of high tensile strength arcano-metallic alloys."

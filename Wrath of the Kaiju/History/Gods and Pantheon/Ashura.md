
The three-faced, six-armed demon god of destruction cast down from the heavens by the deities he offended. Ashura 


## Abyssal Text from the [[Fists of Ashura Monastery]]
- The abyssal text details the hidden story of Ashura, a god of great might and power. He grew fat on his pride, and challenged all other gods to face him in a battle of strength. Over the next 100 years Ashura held a tournament of the gods, challenging each one to approach and attempt to defeat him. One by one they fell before him, until a small of form goddess with 6 arms challenged him. Her name was Tennen, and she stroked Ashura's ego, telling him over and over how strong he truly was. In that time she managed to lull him into a state of boasting and complacency, when she struck a single blow to the back of his head. In that moment Ashura fell, stripped of all of his titles and royal garb. It is believed Ashura walks among the living now, in one form or another.

## Kira

Kira is a Joroguma who has been secretly been stealing away family members of those visiting the graves. She offers any travelers a place to rest, in her home, as well as food and music if they would like, anything to ease their sorrow and grief. The recent earth quakes have caused the tree to split, which has a small opening that leads deeper down into Kira's lair. The other entrance is hidden in her woodshop behind a false panel in the back of a wardrobe. Kira will offer to house the players and give them a place to rest as well as any supplies they may want. 

If the player's happen to have a dead party member with them, she will show them to an empty plot of land, hoping that 
Her house is a fairly large cottage with a guest quarters on the second floor, a total of 3 rooms, and a large hearth on the first. Kira has a wood working area where she constructs any repairs to gravestones or pathways. She also has a harp in the hearth area that she will play during dinner. 

She serves hearty vegetable stews made from tubers she finds around the tree. She charges the adventurers nothing, but many will leave her donations for upkeep of the graveyard.
She has been amassing hundreds of kills over the years, her lair a large subterrain hole below the tree with the sacred tablet of Lei Shan and the skeletons of her past kills. 

She will tell the players that she has seen some large spider like creatures on the forests edge ever since the earth earthquakes, and warns them to be careful. 

If the players sleep in her home, she will attempt to sneak into one of their rooms and charm one of them luring them into her lair, so she may feast.

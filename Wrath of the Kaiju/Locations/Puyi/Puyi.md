The primary metropolitan center of Shen Ji, Puyi serves as the hub of trade and culture in the realm. Puyi is walled city, erected by the last great emperor of Shen Ji, [[Lei Shen]]. The city now serves as the the nexus for political and economic production, with an outlet to the sea along the Imini River, fed from the Kitikami Mountains. 

The city of Puyi was originally founded during lei Shen's rule and served as the "Rome" of his rule. The city was used to establish a cultural hub and served as the major nexus point for the gathering of the armies under his rule. As such the original ancient city was surrounded by large walls and borders the Imini river which runs both through and aside the city. From here Lei shen built the docks that allowed for ship building that later defined the northern coast, the umigiri coast as the ships left their inland post. 

Modern day Puyi is governed by a city council, a primary body of 4 Mildred Vex of Mildreds Magical MagicBox who represents the interest of the merchants of the city, the dean of the college of Hanabi, Ryu the head Chronicler at the Library of the Chroniclers, and Captain Georgio (High level Cleric) , First plume of the Pegasus Guard of Puyi.  This city council votes and discusses the usage of Puyis resources, representing each of the relevant factions of the city. The council rotates responsibly for presenting and speaking to the public regarding public events, and also each carry with them a advisory council of 2 people. The advisory council is also allowed to vote during major decisions but doesn't not speak for the group, the idea being that an individual council member cannot corrupt the decisions of any given faction. These council members are chosen by the other members of the council.

# 3 Unique Things

1. The city of Puyi has aqueducts that move water throughout the city. Many of the aqueducts also have multipurpose of carrying small boats that delivery packages and letters serving as both a distribution system and a water system. The Aqueducts have been enchanted by the college of Hanabi to control flow direction by using bernoullis eq to decrease nozzle size on either aquifer hub. The city lies on a large vertical axis, the "ground" floor of puyi is level with the top of the large wall, with the guts lying directly underneath. 
2.  The city of Puyi has an augmented time control due to the work of the Chronomancer's. The richer area of town, near the promenade, and the moor of merchants has a bubble of arcane runes that cause time to pass 30% slower in that area of the city. 
3. The city is surrounded by an ancient stone wall that has stood for thousands of years.

5d6 per player. 

**1. Secretly Roll**  
All players shake their 5 dice in their cups and slam them face-down on the table. Peek under your cup to see your roll, but keep it hidden from everyone else. 

**2. Place an Opening Bid**  
The first player bids on how many dice of a specific face value exist **across the entire table combined** (not just in their own cup). 

- _Example_: You have two 4s. You guess that other players have some too, so you bid _"There are at least four 4s on the table"_. 
**3. Continue Bidding (Clockwise)**  
Each subsequent player must either **raise the current bid** or **challenge it**. To raise a bid, you must: 

- Increase the **quantity** of any face value (e.g., from _four 4s_ to _five 2s_ or _five 4s_).
- Increase the **face value** while keeping the quantity the same (e.g., from _four 4s_ to _four 5s_). 
**4. Challenge the Bid ("Liar!")**  
If a player makes a bid that you believe is completely impossible, you can shout **"Liar!"** or **"Challenge!"** on your turn. This immediately ends the round. All players lift their cups to count the total matching dice. 
- **If the bid was a lie** (there are _fewer_ matching dice than the bid), the **bidder loses one die**.
- **If the bid was true** (there are _equal to or more_ matching dice than the bid), the **challenger loses one die**. 
**5. Start a New Round**  
Any player who loses all 5 of their dice is permanently eliminated from the game. The person who just lost a die starts the next round by rolling and making a new opening bid

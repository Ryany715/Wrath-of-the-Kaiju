What to say about the Chroniclers, from court scribes to adventurous archaeologists, The Chroniclers provide a service to all people of the Realms: objective record-keeping!! All chroniclers take an oath of honesty and secrecy; they cannot tell a lie and, if asked, will keep secret anything they observe for a period of fifty years, recording the information on sheets of psychic paper that only the writer can read. To eject a chronicler from one’s presence is at best an admission of a lack of transparency, and at worst a confession of one’s will to commit deception.

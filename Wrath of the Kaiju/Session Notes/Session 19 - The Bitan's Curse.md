*   The party set off a sleeping gas trap on a hidden trapdoor in the smugglers' warehouse, and Mon prying it open triggered a small explosion.

## Session Overview

The party descended into the hideout beneath the smugglers' warehouse, finding a large metal door at its base. They attempted to negotiate with the Silk Purse arms dealers inside for information about the curse afflicting the cove, but the negotiations went poorly, and the party split up to pursue different leads across town. Muchu found a fisherman's shack on the outskirts of Yusha Village, where an old man named Rick told him the story of an encounter with a bitan — including the riddle displayed in the water, and the sacred, honored rite of the fisherman chosen to finally claim the bitan's life. Rick explained that if the bitan's bones aren't returned to the sea, the shepherd of the waters, a kaiju named Iminada, will be greatly displeased, and that returning the bones would likely quell her wrath. He also suspected the Mistraider pirates were behind the theft, believed to be holed up in Akibatsu Cove. The party decided to set out for the cove, commandeering a boat from the docks and reluctantly enlisting Rick as captain. On the way, Rick detailed further mysteries of the coast: a lighthouse said to shepherd the dead into the afterlife, and a whirlpool leading to a sunken temple deep beneath the waves.

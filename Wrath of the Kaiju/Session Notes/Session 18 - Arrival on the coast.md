## Recap from Last Session
*   The party uncovered Diloophi's mutiny plot and pushed him overboard, unable to swim.

## Session Overview

Deciding not to let Diloophi drown, the party pulled him back into the dinghy and warned him that his mutiny would not stand, threatening to expose him if he didn't stay aboard the dinghy off the coast. Diloophi cried and pleaded, but the party left him behind and returned to the ship. They eventually reached the Umigiri Coast, arriving at Yusha Village, where they found Kalla Chiyu, a healer from the Heirs of Kirin, tending to an entire village of sick people in the townhall. Rushing in to help, the party heard a long, drawn-out song of mourning that Mirran realized was resonating through the water itself — Aquan runes spelling out a message letter by letter. Searching the village for signs of strange or unnatural skeletons (including Muchu casting *find objects*) turned up nothing, so the party headed to a house they'd previously identified as a smugglers' warehouse, used to move crates of arcanomechanical suits out of the city. Nisuke found and navigated six traps in the room, leading the party to a trapped trapdoor. After accidentally setting off a sleeping gas trap on the door, they had Mon pry it open — triggering a small explosion.

## Session Overview

Mon, the Dr and their newly found friend Asulei battled out in a game of dice with the crew of Captain Sawtooth. Winning a hand of Pirate's dice, the party earned themselves an audience with the Captain. Muchu headed to the Palace of Dreams and set a meeting with the Matron later that night as well. After reuniting in the rest of the party in the Moor of merchants, they decided to kill some time by heading to the library of the Chroniclers. During the research, Chris Tayshin had a strange vision. The swords found nothing in the library of either themselves, or reports of the ghost pirates that seem to be plaguing the supply routes. The party then split into 2 groups, Muchu, Mirran and Chris Tayshin heading to the Palace of dreams for the meeting with the Matron. Asulei, the Dr, Mon and Nisuke headed to their meeting with Captain Sawtooth. At the Palace of Dreams Chris Tayshin went on a vision quest while Muchu and Mirran went to the Matron and received answers to some questions. Muchu found that what he is seeking is in the college spire of metallurgy, but is hidden in some way and not as it seems. He found as well that the armband he has is an artifact of Ashura and carries divine power, that once reunited with all the splinters of Ashura will restore them to full power. Mirran was then giving an answer to a question of her choice, which she took in secret. Chris Tayshin had a strange vision of of himself in various different bodies, each tattooed with a strange symbol facing down great beasts. ![[Chris Tayshin's vision.png|480]]
The rest of the swords met with Captain Sawtooth,  who told them of the ghost pirates that have been plauging the trade routes between the city of Puyi and the Umigiri  coast. He described that his crew has been using bait ships full of crew to protect the supply ships and have the pirates attack the decoys. He agreed to a contract with the swords, that he agreed to pay upon services rendered for them investigating the source of the ghost pirates. Before signing the contract in his blood, he transformed into a hammerhead wereshark satating the swords are now "part of the crew."

He also learned that his armband is an artifact of Ashura carrying divine power — one that, once reunited with all of Ashura's other splinters, will restore Ashura to full power.

*   Before signing the contract in his own blood, Sawtooth transformed into a hammerhead wereshark, declaring the party now "part of the crew."

*   **The Armband of Ashura:** Muchu's armband is confirmed to be an artifact of Ashura, carrying divine power that will help restore Ashura to full strength once reunited with his other splinters — and what Muchu seeks is hidden within the college spire of metallurgy.
*   **Mirran's Secret:** She received an answer from the Matron that she's chosen not to share with the rest of the party.
*   **Chris Tayshin's Vision:** A recurring motif of himself in many bodies, each tattooed with the same strange symbol, facing down great beasts — meaning not yet clear.
*   **The Ghost Pirates:** Confirmed to be plaguing the trade routes between Puyi and the Umigiri Coast; Captain Sawtooth has contracted the party to investigate their source.
*   **A New Ally:** Asulei has joined the party's circle, first appearing at the Dockyards.

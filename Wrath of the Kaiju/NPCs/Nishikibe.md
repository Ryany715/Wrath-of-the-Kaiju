# Nishikibe

## Description
*   **Appearance:** 
![[Wrath of the Kaiju/NPCs/Pictures/Nishikibe.png]]
*   **Personality:** Nishikibe’s a careful and observant man. He is confident and commanding, seemingly knowing people's intentions before they even divulge them. 
*   **Mannerisms:** Nishikibe chooses his words precisely and has a calm, measured cadence in all circumstances

## Background
Nishikibe came to [[Wrath of the Kaiju/Locations/Tetsumine Basin/Tetsumine Basin]] trying to buy [[Chuji Firestorm]] out of her proposed ore contract. After not being able to buy her out, he offered a 50,000-gold bounty on Chuji Firestorm's head; offered to Nisuke. Nishikibe later struck down Kofra Esleufan during the night — Kofra had "ding-dong ditched" his door out of curiosity, and when he returned to knock a second time, Nishikibe opened the door, incapacitated him with a cone of cold and shocking grasp combo, dragged him inside, and slit his throat. The rest of the party found Kofra's corpse stuffed in a trunk. The party de-escalated rather than immediately retaliating, retrieved Kofra's body and armor, and fled town for Puyi.

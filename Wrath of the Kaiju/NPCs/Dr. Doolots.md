# Dr. Doolots

![[Dr Doolots.png]]

## Description
*   **Appearance:** A golden dragonborn with strange, vial-like locks of bluish liquid in place of hair.
*   **Personality:** Inventive and dedicated — repurposed an existing device in an attempt to solve Kihoso's acidification crisis, and graciously accepted the party's help.
*   **Mannerisms:** 

## Background
The party first met Dr. Doolots at the Horn Pits, where she explained she'd repurposed a device to neutralize the acidic aquifer beneath Kihoso, believing it could work on the entire basin. She accompanied the party on the long descent to the aquifer itself, explaining along the way that the trip would take an indeterminate amount of time. At the aquifer, she confirmed the device should work but would need about 12 hours to take full effect — a timeline cut short by the emergence of Nomi, the kaiju actually responsible for the acidification.

After Nomi's defeat, Dr. Doolots continued to brief the party on developments in the region, including rumors of disturbances in the Tetsumine Basin tied to Lei Shen, the Thunder King.

## Motivations & Goals
To solve Kihoso's water crisis through her engineering expertise, and more broadly to understand and address the region's growing environmental/kaiju-related disturbances.

## Background
Fred is a kappa the party encountered resting in a small forest pond on the road to the Tree of Life. He's stuck there, afraid that venturing too far from his pond will cause his water to dry out, and doesn't know the location of the nearest large body of water. The swords of kihoso executed him because they found him "annoying". 

## Motivations & Goals
To protect his small pond and its fish population, and to find a way to a larger body of water someday without risking drying out along the way.

# Captain Totsuka

## Description
*   **Appearance:**
![[Wrath of the Kaiju/NPCs/Pictures/Captain Totsuka.png]]
*   **Personality:** Concerned and amicable, she is primarily concerned with helping the people of the region in the wake of the disaster caused by the Kaiju
*   **Mannerisms:** Totsuka listens far more than she speaks . When she does speak, it is with a rich bass that commands attention . Her words are seldom trivial and always cut to the core of the matter at hand . In an army, she would be respected by superiors and subordinates alike . While listening, she will offer a seat and tea, anticipating the needs of those with whom she speaks well in advance of them becoming needs

## Background
Captain Totsuka was encountered in the ringlander town within the Tetsumine Basin. She first asked the party for aid investigating monks who went missing after sailing for the Ironspire, and warned them of the incoming Rising Blossoms and the Silk Purse. After the party's expedition to the Ironspire, he paid them on behalf of the Heirs of Kirin and shared a dinner with them, during which Chuji Firestorm's ties to the Rising Blossom were uncovered. 

## Motivations & Goals
To protect the ringlander town and the wider region from the growing threats of the Rising Blossoms, the Silk Purse, and the unrest around the Ironspire.

## Background
Captain Geo, 7th Plume of the 7th Battalion of the Pegasus Guard, met the party while they crossed the Thunder Plains toward Puyi. He warned them of a roaming warlord named Volsung Fa, and told them of two notable locations: the Evernight Gate (where mangled traveler remains have been found) and Thunderbird Point (home to massive elemental birds).

## Motivations & Goals
To protect travelers and the city of Puyi from regional threats, including Volsung Fa's growing influence.

# Bombuku

## Description
*   **Appearance:** 
![[Bombuku.png]]
*   **Personality:** Curious and resourceful 
*   **Mannerisms:** Professor Bombuku’s voice is high-pitched, and he has a habit of chewing on the first sound of a sentence as he organises his thoughts . His speech is precise and stretches from the crass to the eloquent . He is as likely to say “well that’s a pile of shit” as “I believe the amplitude of the oscillations was augmented by constructive interference . That is to say, it went boom .”

## Background
Bombuku was roginally met accompanying [[Nisuki]] to the city of [[Wrath of the Kaiju/Locations/Tofuoka Hills/Kihoso]].  He gave them a basalt bottle and asked for an acid sample from the regions after hearing tell of its strange properties. 

Much later, in Puyi, Bombuku was overheard at the College of Hanabi discussing succession plans with Dean Carbone, and detailing research (alongside Carbone) into a self-healing material sought from a kaiju in the Pondermoss Weald. Bombuku implored and employed Nisuke and [[Chris Tacian]] to seek out this material on behalf of the college. 

## Motivations & Goals
Academic research and improvement of the arcanomechanical designs of the region of Puyi
